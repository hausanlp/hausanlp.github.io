About HausaNLP

An open community advancing language AI for Hausa and African languages.

Our Mission

HausaNLP is an open community of academics, researchers, linguists, students, ML engineers and NLP enthusiasts with a shared passion for Hausa natural language processing. We aim to create and provide high-quality resources and models to facilitate Hausa language understanding and generation in the AI community — and to extend this work to the broader African language ecosystem.

Hausa is one of the most widely spoken languages in Africa, with over 120 million first-language speakers. Despite this, it remains severely underrepresented in NLP research. HausaNLP exists to change that.
