What We Work On

From low-resource NLP to multimodal AI — building the full stack of language technology for Africa.

Research Themes

Core Research Areas

Our work spans the breadth of NLP, focused on building foundational resources and state-of-the-art models for Hausa and African languages.

💬
Sentiment & Emotion Analysis

Building annotated corpora and fine-tuned models for sentiment and emotion understanding across African languages and social media text.

AfriSenti NaijaSenti BRIGHTER SemEval-2023 Task 12 SemEval-2025 Task 11
🛡️
Hate Speech & Online Safety

Detecting hate speech, abusive language and online harms in Hausa and other African languages to build safer digital spaces.

AfriHate HERDPhobia HausaHate SemEval-2023 Task 10
🌐
Machine Translation

Advancing translation to and from African languages through high-quality corpora, evaluation metrics and participation in international shared tasks.

AfriMTE AfriCOMET FLORES WMT AFRIDOC-MT IWSLT
🏷️
Named Entity Recognition & POS

Creating annotated datasets and models for named entity recognition and part-of-speech tagging across typologically diverse African languages.

MasakhaNER MasakhaNER 2.0 MasakhaPOS
🖼️
Multimodal NLP

Extending language AI into vision-language tasks — visual question answering, image captioning, and multimodal translation grounded in African contexts.

HaVQA HausaVG ArtELingo-28
📏
Benchmarks & Evaluation

Designing rigorous evaluation frameworks to measure progress of NLP systems across African languages and diverse task types.

IrokoBench AfriQA SemRel2024 INJONGO MasakhaNEWS
Emerging Directions

Frontier Research

New areas where we are pushing the boundaries of African language AI.

🤖
LLM Safety & Alignment

Investigating hallucination detection in multilingual models and advancing safety research for African language contexts — including an OpenAI-funded multilingual mental health safety project.

Mu-SHROOM Hallucination Detection Multilingual Safety

Machine-Generated Text Detection

Identifying AI-generated versus human-written text in Hausa, addressing an important challenge as generative AI becomes more prevalent in African language contexts.

Speech Recognition
