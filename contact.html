FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I contribute to HausaNLP?

We welcome contributions of data, code, and expertise. Check our GitHub repositories and open issues to find ways to contribute.

Can I use HausaNLP datasets in my research?

Yes — all our datasets on HuggingFace are freely available for research use. Please cite the appropriate papers when using them in your work.

Are you looking for student researchers?

Yes! We collaborate with PhD and MSc students. Reach out with your research interests and CV and we will be happy to discuss potential collaboration.

How can my institution collaborate with HausaNLP?

We are open to institutional collaborations. Please contact us with your proposal and we will get back to you within 5 working days.
